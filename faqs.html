Find answers to the most common questions about our smart farming platform.

Farmer Crop Monitoring System is a smart agriculture platform that helps farmers monitor crop health, track weather updates, manage irrigation and analyse farm performance using real-time data and insights.

Open Crop Monitoring from the main menu to see live health scores, growth stage, and soil condition for each field. Sensors and satellite data feed the dashboard automatically, and you'll get an alert if a field needs attention.

The Weather page pulls live conditions and short-term forecasts for your exact field location — temperature, humidity, rainfall, and wind — plus alerts for heavy rain, heat waves, or strong winds so you can plan farm activities ahead of time.

Smart irrigation uses real-time soil moisture and weather data to schedule watering automatically, so crops get exactly the water they need. It can reduce water usage by up to 40% while improving yield consistency.

Go to the Report page and choose a category — Crop, Weather, Irrigation, Soil, or Pest & Disease. From there you can download the report as a PDF, Excel, or CSV file in your preferred format.
